# Service-Specific Receptionist Actions

You are completely right to point this out! Right now, the receptionist just clicks "Call Next" and "Complete" for everything. To make this a truly powerful tool, the receptionist should be able to perform actual tasks depending on what service the customer requested.

## What Services Can a Receptionist Do?

Besides billing, here are some realistic actions a receptionist can do for the other services:

1.  **General Consultation (G)**
    -   **Action:** Collect Initial Vitals / Reason for Visit.
    -   **Details:** The receptionist can ask the customer for a brief reason for their visit, or take basic vitals (weight, blood pressure) before they see the doctor.

2.  **Laboratory (L)**
    -   **Action:** Test Requisition / Barcode Generation.
    -   **Details:** The receptionist selects which specific lab tests the customer needs (e.g., Blood Test, X-Ray) and "generates a lab order".

3.  **Billing (B)**
    -   **Action:** Generate Invoice & Process Payment.
    -   **Details:** (As you suggested) The system will autofill the customer's name and phone number. The receptionist enters the amount and description, and clicks "Generate Bill".

4.  **Pharmacy (P)**
    -   **Action:** Prescription Verification.
    -   **Details:** The receptionist enters a Prescription ID or Medicine List to prepare the order for dispensing.

## Proposed Changes

### [MODIFY] Dashboard.tsx
We will update the "Active Serving Card" in the Receptionist Dashboard. 
When the receptionist clicks **"Start Serving Customer"**, instead of just showing a "Complete" button, a new **Service Action Panel** will slide down.

This panel will dynamically change based on the service:
-   **If Billing:** Show an Invoice Generator form (pre-filled with Customer Name and Phone).
-   **If General:** Show a Vitals/Notes input form.
-   **If Lab:** Show a checklist of common lab tests.
-   **If Pharmacy:** Show a Prescription ID input field.

Once the receptionist fills out the specific form, they click a combined button like **"Generate Bill & Complete Service"** or **"Save Vitals & Complete Service"**.
